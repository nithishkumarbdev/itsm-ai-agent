## Symptoms
- A document is sent to print but nothing comes out.
- The printer does not appear in the list of printers.
- The print queue shows jobs stuck as "Printing" or "Error".
- The printer shows an error light or an out-of-paper, jam or toner message.

## Common Causes
- The wrong printer is selected.
- The device is not connected to the office network, or the VPN is needed and not connected.
- A stuck print job blocking the queue.
- The printer is off, offline, out of paper or toner, or has a paper jam.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check that the correct printer is selected in the print dialog. Office printers are named by floor and location.
2. Check the printer display or lights for messages. Refill paper, clear an obvious paper jam by following the on-screen instructions, and close all covers.
3. Confirm the device is on the office network or connected to the VPN.
4. Open the print queue. Cancel the stuck job, and print a short test page.
5. Turn the printer off, wait 30 seconds, and turn it on again. Wait for it to be ready before printing.
6. Restart the device and try again.
7. Try printing from another device to find out whether the problem is the printer or the user's device.
8. Record the printer name and location, the message on the display, and the results of the test page.

## Restricted Actions
- Do not open the inside of the printer beyond the covers described in the on-screen instructions, and never force a jam out.
- Do not change printer network or security settings.
- Do not install printer drivers from unapproved sources. Use the Software Center.

## When to Escalate
Escalate to Hardware / IT Support if the printer shows a hardware error, needs a technician or repeatedly jams.

Escalate to the Network team if several users cannot reach the same printer.

## Symptoms
- Teams will not open, closes unexpectedly, or shows a blank window.
- The user cannot sign in to Teams.
- Messages do not send or arrive.
- Audio or video does not work in meetings.

## Common Causes
- A poor or unavailable internet connection.
- The app needs to be restarted or updated.
- Sign-in problems caused by a changed password or an unapproved MFA prompt.
- The wrong microphone, speaker or camera is selected.
- A corrupted local app cache.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the device has working internet access.
2. Fully quit Teams, including the icon in the system tray or menu bar, and open it again.
3. If the user cannot sign in, sign out, sign in again with company credentials and approve the MFA prompt. If the MFA prompt does not arrive, follow the MFA Troubleshooting Guide.
4. Check that Teams is up to date. Install any available update from the Software Center or the app's update option.
5. For audio or video problems, open the device settings in Teams and select the correct microphone, speaker and camera. Check that no other application is using them.
6. Try Teams in a web browser. If it works there, the problem is limited to the desktop app.
7. Restart the device.
8. If the app still misbehaves, sign out and clear the Teams cache using the steps in the Software Center support article. Clearing the cache does not delete the user's messages or files.

## Restricted Actions
- Do not uninstall or reinstall Teams using unapproved installers. Use the Software Center.
- Do not change tenant, policy or meeting security settings.
- Do not add a user to teams or channels without an approved request (see Access Management).

## When to Escalate
Escalate to IT Support if the problem continues after the steps above, or if only some users in the same team are affected.

Escalate to Access Management if the user is signed in but cannot see a team or channel they should have access to.

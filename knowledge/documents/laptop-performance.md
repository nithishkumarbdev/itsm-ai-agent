## Symptoms
- The laptop is slow to start up or to open applications.
- Applications freeze or stop responding.
- The fan runs loudly all the time, or the laptop feels very hot.
- The battery drains much faster than usual.

## Common Causes
- Too many applications or browser tabs open at the same time.
- The device has not been restarted for a long time.
- A pending update is waiting to be installed.
- Low free disk space.
- A single application using a large share of CPU or memory.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Ask the user to save their work and restart the laptop. Many performance problems disappear after a restart.
2. Close applications and browser tabs that are not needed.
3. Open the task manager (Windows) or Activity Monitor (Mac) and look for an application using a very high share of CPU or memory. Close it normally. If it will not close, note its name in the ticket.
4. Check free disk space. If less than about 10 percent is free, ask the user to move or archive large files they own, and to empty the recycle bin.
5. Check for pending system updates and install them at a convenient time. Keep the laptop plugged in while updating.
6. Make sure the laptop has air flow: it should be on a hard surface with vents not covered.
7. Note when the slowness started, and whether it happens with every application or only one.

## Restricted Actions
- Do not delete files, folders or user profiles on behalf of the user.
- Do not stop or remove security, management or backup software to improve performance.
- Do not change system or BIOS settings.

## When to Escalate
Escalate to Hardware / IT Support if:
- the laptop is still slow after a restart and the steps above;
- the laptop overheats, shuts down unexpectedly, or shows disk or battery warnings;
- the device is more than a few years old and slow across all applications.

Escalate to Security if an unknown process is using high resources or the user sees unexpected pop-ups.
